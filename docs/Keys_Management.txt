Keys & Tokens Management
------------------------
• App loads keys from ENV first, then config\keys.local.json, then (deprecated) config\keys.json.
• Never logs raw secrets; /integrations/status shows presence (true/false) only.
• To set your keys locally, run: scripts\set_keys.cmd (writes config\keys.local.json).
• Keep keys.local.json private; back it up separately. Support Pack redacts values by default.
