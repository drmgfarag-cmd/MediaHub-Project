# MediaHub User Guide

**Version:** 5.4  
**Last Updated:** October 6, 2025

---

## 1. Welcome to MediaHub!

This guide will walk you through the main features of MediaHub and how to use them.

---

## 2. The Four Pillars

MediaHub is built on four core pillars:

### MediaHub Home
This is your main dashboard, inspired by services like Prime Video and Netflix. It features:
- **Hero Banner:** Highlights important or new media.
- **Carousels:** Browse your media by category, genre, recently added, etc.
- **Omnibox Search:** A powerful global search to find anything in your library.

### Text Editor
MediaHub includes a powerful built-in text editor for managing NFO files, notes, and other text-based documents. Features include:
- **Syntax Highlighting:** For over 50 languages.
- **Macros:** Record and replay common tasks.
- **Bookmarks:** Quickly jump to important sections.

### Downloader
Manage all your downloads in one place. The downloader supports:
- **Download Queue:** See what's currently downloading and what's next.
- **RSS Automation:** Automatically download new media from your favorite RSS feeds.
- **Archive Extraction:** Automatically extract downloaded archives (.zip, .rar).

### Real-Debrid Manager
Integrate with your Real-Debrid account to manage your cloud storage and downloads.
- **Browse Files:** View your Real-Debrid files directly in MediaHub.
- **Bulk Operations:** Download, delete, or move multiple files at once.
- **Filtering:** Filter your files by name, size, or date.

---

## 3. Key Features

### Advanced Deduplication
MediaHub automatically detects and manages duplicate media files.
- **Hash-based detection:** Finds exact duplicates.
- **Criteria-based analysis:** Finds duplicates based on metadata, quality, etc.
- **Bulk actions:** Keep the best version and delete the rest.

### Media Management
- **Automatic Organization:** MediaHub can automatically rename and organize your media files.
- **Metadata Fetching:** Automatically download metadata and artwork from TMDB, IMDB, etc.
- **Collections:** Create manual or smart collections to group your media.
- **Subtitle Downloader:** Automatically download subtitles in your preferred languages.

### Web UI
In addition to the desktop app, you can access MediaHub through a web browser on your local network. This is perfect for:
- **TVs:** Use your smart TV browser to watch media.
- **Mobile Devices:** Browse your library and manage downloads from your phone or tablet.
- **Casting:** Cast media to Chromecast and other devices.

---

## 4. Getting Started

1. **Add Media:** Go to **Settings > Media Folders** to add your media directories.
2. **Scan Media:** MediaHub will automatically scan your folders and fetch metadata.
3. **Browse:** Use the **MediaHub Home** to browse your library.
4. **Watch:** Click on any media item to start playing.

---

## 5. Settings

### Metadata Providers
- Go to **Settings > Metadata Providers** to configure which services to use for metadata (TMDB, IMDB, etc.) and set their priority.

### Subtitle Policy
- Go to **Settings > Subtitle Policy** to configure automatic subtitle downloads and language preferences.

### Integrations
- Go to **Settings > Integrations** to monitor the status of all external services.

### Language
- Go to **Settings > Language** to change the interface language.

---

This guide provides a basic overview. Explore the application to discover all its features! For more details, see the full documentation in the `docs/` directory.

Enjoy MediaHub!
